FRQI Encoding ✅
- Flexible Representation of Quantum Images
- Amplitude-based encoding: θ = 2×arccos(√pixel)
- Fewer qubits (more efficient)
- Faster execution
- Position superposition + color rotation

NEQR Encoding ✅
- Novel Enhanced Quantum Image Representation
- Binary basis state encoding: |color⟩ = |b₁b₂b₃b₄⟩
- More qubits (more precise)
- Better image quality
- Position superposition + color basis states

- Output: Base64-encoded PNG images

- SSIM: Structural Similarity Index

- Fidelity: Quantum state fidelity
- Circuit: Depth, gates, qubits
