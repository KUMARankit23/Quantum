================================================================================
EXPECTED FINDINGS
================================================================================

When you run the experiments, expect to discover:

1. RESOLUTION SCALING
   - Quantum resources scale predictably with image size
   - 2×2: 2 qubits, 4×4: 4 qubits, 8×8: 6 qubits, 16×16: 8 qubits

2. SHOT COUNT IMPACT
   - Quality improves with more measurements
   - Improvement follows logarithmic curve
   - Optimal balance around 1000 shots

3. NOISE SENSITIVITY
   - Even 1% error significantly degrades quality
   - Different noise types have different severity
   - Depolarizing noise most damaging

4. CIRCUIT COMPLEXITY
   - Deeper circuits provide better state preparation
   - Trade-off: quality vs execution time
   - Deeper circuits more noise-sensitive

5. IMAGE REPRESENTATION
   - Different image types reconstruct differently
   - Structured patterns (checkerboard) reconstruct better
   - Random patterns more difficult

6. CLASSICAL ADVANTAGE
   - Classical computing 10-100× faster than quantum
   - Classical quality comparable or better
   - Quantum doesn't provide practical advantage at small scale
