## KEY FINDINGS (Expected)

When experiments run, you'll discover:

1. **Resolution Scaling**: Quantum resources scale predictably
2. **Measurement Trade-off**: Quality improves logarithmically with shots
3. **Noise Problem**: Even 1% error significantly degrades quality
4. **Depth Trade-off**: Deeper circuits = better quality but slower
5. **Pattern Effects**: Different images reconstruct differently
6. **Classical Advantage**: Classical is 10-100× faster at small scale

**Most Important**: Quantum computing doesn't provide practical advantage for image processing at small scale. That's **honest research**, not failure.
