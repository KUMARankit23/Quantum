"Today we're presenting our quantum image processing research project. Our research question is: What determines the performance of a quantum image processing system? Rather than claiming quantum is faster, we systematically measured key factors."

### Quantum Formulation (Show 3D)
"We use amplitude encoding to map pixels to quantum amplitudes. The formula is |ψ⟩ = (1/√Z) Σ √(I_i) |i⟩. When we measure the quantum state, the probabilities become pixel values."

### Methodology
"We implemented 22 Python modules with 5,400 lines of code. We have preprocessing, quantum encoding, circuit execution, reconstruction, and evaluation. This is a complete end-to-end system."

### Experiments
"We designed 6 controlled experiments: resolution scaling, shot count impact, noise sensitivity, circuit complexity, image types, and quantum vs classical. Each experiment varies ONE parameter."

### Results (Click Charts)
"Here we can see resolution scaling - resources grow predictably. Here's shot count - quality improves logarithmically. Noise severely degrades results, and classical is 10-100× faster than quantum."

### Conclusion
"Our honest finding is: quantum doesn't provide practical advantage at small scale. But that's valuable research because it shows what needs to improve for quantum computing to be practical."
