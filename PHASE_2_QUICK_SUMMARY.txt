QUANTUM ENCODING (src/quantum/encoding.py)

Mathematical Representation:
  |ψ⟩ = (1/√Z) Σ √(I_i) |i⟩
  where:
    - I_i: pixel intensity
    - Z: normalization constant
    - |i⟩: computational basis state

  ✓ Normalization verification
