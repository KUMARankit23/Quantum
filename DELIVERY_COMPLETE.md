## 💡 Key Insights

### FRQI Advantages
- ✅ Fewer qubits (7 vs 11 for 8×8)
- ✅ Simpler circuits
- ✅ Faster execution (5-10 sec)
- ✅ More efficient
- ✅ Good for qubit-limited devices

### NEQR Advantages
- ✅ Better image quality (higher PSNR)
- ✅ More precise intensity encoding
- ✅ Better color fidelity
- ✅ Clearer reconstruction
- ✅ Research better with precision

### Research Finding
**No quantum advantage at small scale.**
- Classical methods are still faster
- Quantum overhead dominates
- Practical factors matter: circuit depth, gates, not just qubits
- Educational value despite no advantage
