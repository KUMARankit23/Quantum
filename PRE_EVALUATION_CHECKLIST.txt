===== KEY TALKING POINTS =====

If Asked: "Why Quantum?"
Answer: "Superposition allows parallel feature processing. Amplitude encoding preserves image structure in quantum states. Future potential with larger problems and error correction."

If Asked: "Did Quantum Win?"
Answer: "No, classical is 10-100x faster at this scale. This shows honest research. At larger scale with error correction, quantum could provide advantages."

If Asked: "What's the Main Problem?"
Answer: "Quantum hardware is noisy (0.1-1% errors) and simulation overhead dominates at small scale. Both issues improve with better hardware and error correction."

If Asked about Limitations:
Answer: "We identified zero-pixel encoding issues, noise collapse at extreme levels, scalability at >8 qubits, and measurement destroys state. We have mitigation strategies for each."

If Unsure:
Answer: "That's a great question. Let me think... Based on our analysis in [document], we believe..."
